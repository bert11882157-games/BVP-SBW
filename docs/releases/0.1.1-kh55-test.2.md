# Tu-95 KH-55 test build 2

This testing update combines the stationary ground-vehicle jitter fix from BVP/SBW
`0.9.19beta` with the Tu-95 cruise-missile test configuration.

## Changes

- Includes upstream fix `2eb9819e16525c75662b66b0298259bd7b27cb58`, merged into main as
  `228ef2e`: stationary tracked and wheeled vehicles retain the precise heading through
  matching periodic byte-angle refreshes. Moving corrections, mismatched angle buckets,
  aircraft and provider-owned vehicle poses retain their existing rotation handling.
- Retains eight independent KH-55 belly stations, one coordinate assignment per station,
  normal selected-weapon firing and rejection of shots without valid coordinates.
- Aircraft cruise missiles hold their release altitude through the initial turn and cruise,
  then guide to the target on final approach. KH-55 uses the KH-29 artwork and half the
  base FFA cruise acceleration.
- Retains the Tu-95's 30% roll increase, fast flares, chaff/RWR and radar, along with the
  simplified vehicle terminal and Dominions radar relationships in the matching FFA build.

## Matching versions

Install BVP **0.1.1-kh55-test.2**, Superb Warfare **0.8.9-bvp.3**, and Fire From Above
**1.0.5-bvp.2** on the server and all clients. FFA is unchanged from the preceding cruise
testing build. Keep meshloader **0.1.1**, Dominions **1.0.11 or newer**, and the pack's other
required dependencies. Replace the previous active BVP/SBW JARs.

The SBW archive's `6effe43` filename suffix is the repository's pinned packaging label;
the release's source commit and source inventory identify the complete combined build.

## Validation

The release page records the final source-build checks, focused jitter and armament test
results, and exact artifact hashes. The preceding full SBW test run had five failures in
unchanged areas; this update does not claim to repair them. Minecraft was not launched.

Use [the Tu-95 testing procedure](../TU95_CRUISE_TESTING.md) for weapon acceptance. Also
leave a tracked or wheeled ground vehicle stationary at a non-round heading through repeated
network refreshes, check that its hull and attached sights remain steady, then drive and
turn it to verify moving corrections still apply. In-game and multiplayer acceptance remain
required before treating this testing release as stable.
